Hey, I'm Uche.

Fullstack Blockchain Developer based in Nigeria. Computer Science graduate. I build on both ends of the stack — clean frontends and battle-tested on-chain logic — driven by one principle: necessity is the birthplace of innovation. Every project I've shipped started as a real problem that needed solving, not a feature to impress.

I'm a team player first. I think out loud, move fast, and make sure everyone around me ships too. Good ideas don't live in isolation — they get built, tested, and refined with people who care about the outcome as much as you do. When I'm not writing code, I'm tracking what the web3 ecosystem just broke and figuring out what needs to exist that doesn't yet.
